# Spoken English Curriculum Plan: Zero to Advanced & Professional (for Bangla Speakers)

This plan provides a comprehensive, international-standard spoken English curriculum (**ইংরেজি স্পোকেন কোর্স: শূন্য থেকে অ্যাডভান্সড ও প্রফেশনাল**) designed specifically for Bengali native speakers. The instruction, grammar explanations, pronunciation tips, and cultural bridges are delivered in Bangla, while training the learner toward natural, spontaneous, and fluent English speech.

The framework adheres strictly to the **CEFR** (**A1 → A2 → B1 → B2 → Specialist Pro Tracks**), integrating standards from the **British Council**, **Cambridge Assessment English**, **IELTS Speaking Band 7.5+**, and modern **Global Tech & Workplace Communication**.

---

## 1. Core Principles & Contrastive Analysis (Bangla → English)

### 1.1 Speaking First (Oral Fluency & Automated Reflexes)
1. **Oral Muscle Memory:** Every lesson terminates in active spoken output. Grammar is taught as communicative sentence patterns (chunks & frames), never as isolated rules.
2. **Speed Ladder Repetition:** Offline Android TTS (`en-US` or `en-GB`) powers the multi-speed cadence (`0.8x slow → 1.15x Bangla meaning → 0.9x natural → 1.0x native rhythm → 1200ms pause for learner repetition`).
3. **100% Token Integrity:** All 512 items per level follow strict word-token alignment (`clean(tokens) == clean(target)`), with accurate Bengali phonetic transcription (`bn_pron`).

### 1.2 Overcoming Bengali Speaker Interference (Phonetic & Structural Traps)

Bangla and English belong to different branches of the Indo-European family, leading to persistent pronunciation habits and grammatical transfer errors among Bangladeshi learners. This curriculum targets these exact pain points from Day 1:

| Challenge | Bangla Tendency / Confusion | Accurate English Target & Drill Solution |
|---|---|---|
| **/v/ vs /w/** | Pronouncing both as 'ভ' or both as 'ওয়' (*vine* sounds like *wine*, *very* sounds like *wary*). | **Labiodental /v/:** Upper front teeth touch the lower lip with vocal vibration (*ভ়*).<br>**Bilabial /w/:** Lips round tightly into an 'ও/উ' shape without touching teeth (*ওয়*). |
| **/s/ vs /ʃ/** | In standard Bangla, 'স' is predominantly pronounced as palato-alveolar 'শ' (*sh*). Learners say *shome* (some), *shame* (same), *shee* (see). | Explicit tongue placement drills behind upper teeth for sharp alveolar /s/ (*স*), contrasting minimal pairs (*see/she, seat/sheet, save/shave*). |
| **/θ/ and /ð/ (TH)** | Substituting dental/retroflex 'থ / ঠ' for /θ/ (*thanks, think*) and 'দ' for /ð/ (*this, that, brother*). | Place tongue-tip gently between upper and lower teeth with continuous air release (voiceless /θ/ and voiced /ð/). |
| **/p/ vs /f/** | Replacing unvoiced labiodental /f/ with bilabial aspirated 'ফ' (*pʰ*), or unvoiced English /p/ with unaspirated 'প'. | Distinguish upper-teeth-to-lip /f/ (*food, life*) from dual-lip aspirated English /p/ (*paper, pen*). |
| **/z/ Buzz** | Substituting postalveolar 'জ' (/dʒ/) for voiced alveolar /z/. Learners say *joo* instead of *zoo*, *pleaje* instead of *please*. | Continuous vocal cord vibration with tongue behind front teeth (*জ়*). Drills on plural '-s' and 3rd-person singular endings (*keys, goes, days*). |
| **Stress-Timing vs Syllable-Timing** | Bangla is syllable-timed (equal time per syllable); English is **stress-timed** (rhythm depends on stressed syllables). Learners give every word equal weight, sounding robotic. | Teaching content words vs function words, rhythm beats, and natural reductions: *wanna, gonna, could've, what do you -> whaddya*. |
| **Weak Forms & Schwa (/ə/)** | Pronouncing prepositions and auxiliaries with full vowel sounds (*to* as 'টু', *of* as 'অফ', *can* as 'ক্যান'). | Drills on reduced neutral schwa: *to* /tə/, *of* /əv/, *can* /kən/ in connected speech (*I can do it* = /aɪ kən duː ɪt/). |
| **Silent Letters** | Pronouncing historical silent letters: *debt, doubt, Wednesday, receipt, listen, muscle, salmon, half, foreign*. | Explicit "Silent Letter Spotlights" with audio comparisons. |
| **3rd-Person Singular `-s/-es`** | In Bangla, verbs don't add an extra 's' for third person. Learners habitually say: *"He work here"*, *"She like tea"*. | High-frequency habituation drills: *He lives, she speaks, it works*. |
| **Preposition Traps** | Literal Bangla translation creates common errors: *"listen me"* (listen to), *"good in"* (good at), *"married with"* (married to), *"discuss about"* (discuss). | Contextual collocation frames drilled repeatedly. |

## 3. Detailed Level Syllabi

### 3.1 Level A1: Zero to Foundations (`en-standard-a1`)
**Target:** Absolute beginners and false beginners who can read English letters but freeze when speaking. 
**Goal:** Build foundational sentence patterns, introduce oneself, handle everyday transactions, master present time actions, and eliminate basic pronunciation slips.

| Unit | Title (English / Bangla) | Core Spoken Focus & Grammar | Speaking Output (Can-Do) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **U01** | First Meetings & Greetings<br>(প্রথম পরিচয় ও শুভেচ্ছা) | Hello/Hi, Introductions, Be-verbs (*am/is/are*), Titles (*Mr./Ms.*), Asking *"How are you?"* | Introduce yourself with name, origin, and profession in natural flowing English. |
| **U02** | Personal Info & Country<br>(নিজের পরিচয় ও দেশ) | Origin (*I come from / I am from*), Nationality, Living location, Age, Phone & Email spelling. | Share detailed contact info and hometown background clearly. |
| **U03** | Family, Friends & People<br>(পরিবার, আত্মীয় ও বন্ধু) | Possessives (*my, your, his, her, their*), Family members, Describing marital status & siblings. | Describe immediate family members and relationships. |
| **U04** | Numbers, Prices & Shopping<br>(সংখ্যা, দাম ও কেনাকাটা) | Numbers 1–1000, Currency ($/£/৳), *"How much is this?"*, Payment methods (*cash or card*). | Buy groceries and everyday items, ask prices, and negotiate politely. |
| **U05** | Daily Routines & Habits<br>(দৈনন্দিন রুটিন ও অভ্যাস) | Present Simple (*I wake up, I go*), Time adverbs (*always, usually, sometimes, never*), Times of day. | Narrate a complete morning-to-night routine smoothly. |
| **U06** | Telling Time, Days & Dates<br>(সময়, দিন ও তারিখ) | Clock times (*half past, quarter to*), Days of week, Months, Prepositions of time (*at, on, in*). | Schedule appointments, ask and tell the exact time. |
| **U07** | Actions Right Now (Present Continuous)<br>(এই মুহূর্তে চলমান কাজ) | *Am/is/are + verb-ing*, Continuous questions (*What are you doing?*), Temporary states. | Describe live ongoing events and activities on a phone call. |
| **U08** | Asking WH Questions<br>(WH দিয়ে সঠিক প্রশ্ন করা) | *What, Where, When, Why, Who, How*, Question intonation, Auxiliary inversion (*Where do you live?*). | Formulate direct, grammatically flawless questions without hesitation. |
| **U09** | Food, Drinks & Dining Out<br>(খাবার, পানীয় ও রেস্তোরাঁ) | Food vocabulary, Ordering (*I would like / Can I have?*), Expressing likes & dislikes. | Order a meal, ask for the bill, and express food preferences in a restaurant. |
| **U10** | Home, Rooms & Daily Objects<br>(বাড়ি, ঘর ও প্রয়োজনীয় জিনিস) | *There is / There are*, Furniture, Household items, Prepositions of place (*on, in, under, next to*). | Describe an apartment or bedroom and locate misplaced household items. |
| **U11** | City Navigation & Directions<br>(শহরের পথঘাট ও দিকনির্দেশনা) | Asking directions (*Excuse me, where is the...?*), *Turn left, go straight, cross the street*. | Ask for and give street directions to tourists or taxi drivers. |
| **U12** | Likes, Dislikes & Free Time<br>(পছন্দ-অপছন্দ ও অবসর) | *Like / Love / Enjoy / Hate + -ing*, Hobbies, Weekend activities, Expressing interest. | Converse comfortably about hobbies, sports, and favorite pastimes. |
| **U13** | Ability & Requests with "Can"<br>(সক্ষমতা ও অনুরোধ: Can) | Modal verb *can / can't*, Polite requests (*Can you help me?*), Asking for permission. | Ask for favors, offer assistance, and state skills (*I can speak English*). |
| **U14** | Describing People & Appearance<br>(মানুষের চেহারা ও বৈশিষ্ট্য) | Adjectives of appearance (tall, short, slim), Hair, Clothing (*He is wearing a blue shirt*). | Describe a person's physical features and outfit accurately. |
| **U15** | Weather, Seasons & Small Talk<br>(আবহাওয়া ও প্রাথমিক কুশলবিনিময়) | Weather conditions (*sunny, rainy, chilly*), Bangladesh 6 seasons vs Western 4 seasons. | Initiate casual small talk using weather and temperature observations. |
| **U16** | A1 Fluency Capstone & Review<br>(লেভেল A1 পূর্ণাঙ্গ রিভিশন ও টেস্ট) | Comprehensive role-play, Pronunciation recap (V/W, S/Sh, TH), Can-Do speaking assessment. | Pass the complete A1 speaking simulation with 100% natural conversational reflex. |

### 3.2 Level A2: Elementary Fluency & Narrative Life (`en-standard-a2`)
**Target:** Learners who know basic English but struggle to tell stories about past events, discuss future plans, or handle practical travel, healthcare, and office scenarios.

| Unit | Title (English / Bangla) | Core Spoken Focus & Grammar | Speaking Output (Can-Do) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **U01** | The Past with Was & Were<br>(অতীতের অবস্থা: Was/Were) | Past states of being (*I was at home, They were late*), Question forms (*Where were you yesterday?*). | Describe past states, whereabouts, and childhood memories. |
| **U02** | Past Actions: Regular Verbs<br>(অতীতের কাজ: নিয়মিত ভার্ব) | Regular past tense (*-ed* pronunciation: /t/, /d/, /ɪd/), Time markers (*yesterday, last week, ago*). | Narrate completed actions with correct *-ed* phonetic endings. |
| **U03** | Past Actions: Irregular Verbs<br>(অতীতের কাজ: অনিয়মিত ভার্ব) | Top 50 irregular verbs (*went, bought, saw, ate, took, had*), Negative past (*didn't + base verb*). | Tell a fluid story about last weekend or a previous vacation. |
| **U04** | Past Continuous & Interrupted Actions<br>(অতীতের চলমান কাজ: Past Continuous) | *Was/were + -ing*, Combining with *when/while* (*I was cooking when the phone rang*). | Explain circumstances surrounding an accident or sudden incident. |
| **U05** | Future Plans: Going to & Will<br>(ভবিষ্যতের পরিকল্পনা: Going to ও Will) | Planned intentions (*I am going to start a course*) vs Spontaneous decisions (*I will help you*). | Discuss personal goals, weekend trips, and spontaneous offers. |
| **U06** | Making Suggestions & Invitations<br>(প্রস্তাব ও নিমন্ত্রণ জানানো) | *Let's, Why don't we, Shall we, How about + -ing*, Accepting and declining politely with reasons. | Invite friends or colleagues to social outings and respond graciously. |
| **U07** | Obligation & Rules: Must & Have to<br>(বাধ্যবাধকতা ও নিয়ম: Must/Have to) | Expressing strong obligation (*have to / must*), Lack of obligation (*don't have to*), Prohibition. | Explain workplace rules, traffic laws, and daily responsibilities. |
| **U08** | Advice & Suggestions: Should & Ought to<br>(পরামর্শ দেওয়া ও নেওয়া: Should) | Giving constructive advice (*You should take rest*), Asking for advice (*What should I do?*). | Advise friends on health, study techniques, and lifestyle changes. |
| **U09** | Health, Illness & At the Doctor<br>(স্বাস্থ্য, অসুস্থতা ও ডাক্তারের কাছে) | Symptoms (*headache, fever, sore throat*), Explaining pain, Pharmacy prescriptions. | Explain symptoms to a doctor or pharmacist clearly and follow instructions. |
| **U10** | Hotel, Booking & Check-in<br>(হোটেল বুকিং ও চেক-ইন) | Reserving a room, Special requests (*non-smoking, high floor*), Check-in/check-out dialogue. | Book accommodation, check in at a hotel desk, and report room issues. |
| **U11** | Travel, Airport & Transit<br>(ভ্রমণ, বিমানবন্দর ও ট্রানজিট) | Airport check-in, Baggage allowance, Boarding gates, Asking flight departure info. | Navigate international airports, check in luggage, and clear immigration basics. |
| **U12** | Comparatives & Superlatives<br>(তুলনা করা: ভালো, আরো ভালো, সেরা) | Comparing two things (*bigger than, more expensive than*), Superlatives (*the best, the tallest*). | Compare gadgets, transport options, and living costs in different cities. |
| **U13** | Shopping for Clothes & Sizes<br>(পোশাক ও কেনাকাটা: সাইজ ও ট্রায়াল) | Asking for sizes (*medium, large*), Trying on clothes (*fitting room*), Requesting discounts. | Shop for garments, ask to try clothes on, and handle exchange requests. |
| **U14** | Telephoning & Leaving Messages<br>(ফোনে কথা বলা ও বার্তা রাখা) | Answering calls, Asking for someone (*May I speak to...?*), Leaving and taking phone messages. | Conduct basic phone conversations and record voice messages accurately. |
| **U15** | Daily Workplace Communication<br>(কর্মক্ষেত্রে নিত্যদিনের কথা) | Talking to teammates, Asking for help, Discussing deadlines, Attending simple morning standups. | Participate actively in basic office interactions and routine status updates. |
| **U16** | A2 Fluency Capstone & Review<br>(লেভেল A2 পূর্ণাঙ্গ রিভিশন ও টেস্ট) | Narrative speaking task (3-minute personal story), Situational role-plays, A2 Speaking Test. | Achieve spontaneous elementary fluency across all practical life domains. |

### 3.3 Level B1: Intermediate Autonomy & Conversational Ease (`en-standard-b1`)
**Target:** Learners who have grammar knowledge but struggle with spontaneous conversation, debate, expressing nuanced views, or using complex sentence connectors.

| Unit | Title (English / Bangla) | Core Spoken Focus & Grammar | Speaking Output (Can-Do) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **U01** | Present Perfect: Life Experiences<br>(জীবনের অভিজ্ঞতা প্রকাশ: Present Perfect) | *Have/has + V3*, Use with *ever, never, already, yet*, Contrast with Simple Past. | Share travel, career, and personal milestones spontaneously. |
| **U02** | Present Perfect Continuous: Duration<br>(কাজের ধারাবাহিকতা ও সময়কাল) | *Have been + -ing*, Using *since* vs *for*, Expressing ongoing life projects. | Explain how long you have been working, studying, or living somewhere. |
| **U03** | Expressing Opinions & Nuance<br>(মতামত প্রকাশ ও দৃষ্টিভঙ্গি) | *In my opinion, As far as I'm concerned, From my perspective, I strongly believe that...*. | Express well-reasoned personal views on social, tech, and cultural topics. |
| **U04** | Agreeing, Disagreeing & Diplomacy<br>(সহমত, দ্বিমত ও ভদ্রভাবে ভিন্নমত) | Partial agreement (*I see your point, but...*), Polite disagreement without being rude. | Engage in polite debates without causing friction or sounding aggressive. |
| **U05** | Real Conditionals (Zero & First)<br>(বাস্তব শর্তমূলক বাক্য: First Conditional) | *If + present, will + base verb*, Time clauses (*when, as soon as, unless*). | Discuss possibilities, future consequences, and conditions clearly. |
| **U06** | Imaginary Conditionals (Second)<br>(কাল্পনিক পরিস্থিতি: Second Conditional) | *If I were you, I would...*, *If I had more time, I would learn coding*. | Speculate about dream jobs, lottery winnings, and give friendly advice. |
| **U07** | Passive Voice in Daily Life<br>(দৈনন্দিন জীবনে প্যাসিভ ভয়েসের ব্যবহার) | Agentless passive (*The package was delivered, English is spoken here*). | Describe processes, report news events, and announce completed tasks. |
| **U08** | Reported Speech & Indirect Quotes<br>(অন্যের কথা উদ্ধৃত করা: Reported Speech) | Backshift of tenses (*He said he was busy, She told me that she had finished*). | Relay messages, summarize meetings, and repeat conversations accurately. |
| **U09** | Discourse Markers & Connectors<br>(বাক্যের সংযোগকারী শব্দ ও ধারাবাহিকতা) | *However, Although, Therefore, Furthermore, On the other hand, In spite of*. | Construct cohesive multi-sentence arguments with natural transition phrases. |
| **U10** | Handling Complaints & Problems<br>(অভিযোগ ও সমস্যার সমাধান) | Expressing dissatisfaction politely (*I'm afraid there is a problem with...*), Demanding remedies. | Resolve customer service disputes, flight delays, and defective product claims. |
| **U11** | Storytelling & Narrative Tenses<br>(গল্প বলা ও অতীতের ধারাবাহিক বর্ণনা) | Combining Past Simple, Past Continuous & Past Perfect (*When I arrived, the train had left*). | Deliver an engaging 2-minute personal anecdote with dramatic flair. |
| **U12** | Expressing Certainty & Deduction<br>(সম্ভাবনা ও অনুমান: Must be, Might be) | Modals of deduction (*It must be true, He can't be at home, They might arrive late*). | Analyze ambiguous situations and deduce probable causes logically. |
| **U13** | Phrasal Verbs in Spoken Action<br>(কথোপকথনে বহুল ব্যবহৃত ফ্রেজাল ভার্ব) | Top 30 communicative phrasal verbs (*look after, call off, figure out, give up, run out of*). | Use casual conversational phrasal verbs naturally like a native speaker. |
| **U14** | Small Talk & Social Networking<br>(সামাজিক আড্ডা ও নেটওয়ার্কিং) | Ice-breakers, Keeping conversations going (*Echo questions, rejoinders: Really? That's great!*). | Build rapport with strangers at events, meetups, and coffee breaks. |
| **U15** | Structuring a 3-Minute Presentation<br>(৩ মিনিটের প্রেজেন্টেশন ও উপস্থাপনা) | Signposting language (*To begin with, Moving on to, In conclusion*), Audience engagement. | Deliver a well-structured short presentation on a chosen topic. |
| **U16** | B1 Fluency Capstone & Review<br>(লেভেল B1 পূর্ণাঙ্গ রিভিশন ও টেস্ট) | Spontaneous conversation test, Fluency check, B1 Intermediate Speaking Certificate. | Speak independently on familiar topics without noticeable searching for words. |

### 3.4 Level B2: Corporate, Presentation & Career Mastery (`en-standard-b2`)
**Target:** Ambitious professionals, software engineers, managers, and graduates preparing for MNC job interviews, client pitches, board meetings, and high-stakes international communication.

## 4. Item Schema & Phonetic Standard

Each item across all 5 levels conforms to the standard engine format:

```json
{
  "id": "en-a1-01-l01-01",
  "concept_id": "en_a1_hello_introduction",
  "type": "sentence",
  "level": "A1",
  "target": "Hello, my name is Rahim and I am from Dhaka.",
  "bn": "হ্যালো, আমার নাম রহিম এবং আমি ঢাকা থেকে এসেছি।",
  "bn_pron": "হ্যালো, মাই নেইম ইজ রাহিম অ্যান্ড আই অ্যাম ফ্রম ঢাকা।",
  "tokens": [
    {"target": "Hello,", "bn": "হ্যালো,"},
    {"target": "my", "bn": "আমার"},
    {"target": "name", "bn": "নাম"},
    {"target": "is", "bn": "হয়"},
    {"target": "Rahim", "bn": "রহিম"},
    {"target": "and", "bn": "এবং"},
    {"target": "I", "bn": "আমি"},
    {"target": "am", "bn": "হই"},
    {"target": "from", "bn": "থেকে"},
    {"target": "Dhaka.", "bn": "ঢাকা।"}
  ],
  "tags": ["A1", "Greetings", "Introductions"],
  "status": "active"
}
```

### 4.1 Strict Quality Gates
1. **Token Invariant:** `clean(" ".join(t["target"] for t in tokens)) == clean(target)` must pass with zero tolerance.
2. **Bengali Phonetics (`bn_pron`):** Every target string has an accurate phonetic transcription in Bengali script, capturing vowel quality, diphthongs, and English consonant contrasts.
3. **Speed Ladder:**
   - English TTS plays at `0.8x` (clear slow articulation).
   - Bangla voice plays at `1.15x` (crisp explanation).
   - English TTS repeats at `0.9x` (natural pace).
   - English TTS completes at `1.0x` (native tempo).
   - `1200ms` pause for learner vocal repetition.
