# Spoken Mandarin Chinese Curriculum Plan: Zero to HSK 4 & China Trade (for Bangla Speakers)

This plan is for a comprehensive spoken Mandarin Chinese course (**চাইনিজ (ম্যান্ডারিন) স্পোকেন ও পিনইন কোর্স: শূন্য থেকে HSK 4 ও বিজনেস প্রো**) built for Bangladeshi learners. The base language of instruction is Bangla, using **Simplified Chinese (`简体字`, Mandarin Putonghua / `zh-CN`)**, **Pinyin + 4-Tone System**, and a high-precision **Bangla Phonetic Bridge (`bn_pron`)**.

All changes are strictly additive and isolated via the `zh-` ID prefix and `chinese_course` key—existing courses (English, Japanese, Urdu, Hindi, German, Regional Bangla, and Quran) remain untouched.

## 1. Pedagogical Principles (Bangla ➔ Mandarin Chinese)

### 1.1 Leverage What Bangla Already Gives Learners
1. **Zero Verb Conjugation & Zero Grammatical Gender:**
   - Unlike German, Hindi, or Urdu, Chinese verbs **never conjugate** for person, gender, or tense!
   - *Bangla:* আমি খাই / সে খায় / তারা খাবে ➔ *Chinese:* `Wǒ chī` / `Tā chī` / `Tāmen yào chī` — the verb `吃 (chī)` stays 100% identical everywhere!
2. **Direct Classifier / Measure Word Match (`量词`):**
   - Bangla naturally pairs numbers with classifiers (`একটা বই`, `দুইজন লোক`, `তিনখানা টিকিট`). Chinese works the exact same way:
     - `একটা ফোন` ➔ `一部手机` (`yí bù shǒujī`)
     - `দুইজন মানুষ` ➔ `两个人` (`liǎng gè rén`)
     - `তিনটা বই` ➔ `三本书` (`sān běn shū`)
3. **Sentence-Final Question Particle `吗 (ma)` = Bangla "কি?":**
   - *Bangla:* আপনি ভালো আছেন **কি**? ➔ *Chinese:* `Nǐ hǎo ma?` (`你好吗？`) — just append `吗` at the end of any statement to make it a yes/no question!
4. **Logical Calendar & Number System:**
   - Chinese months are literally numbered `1-month` to `12-month` (`一月` = জানুয়ারি, `二月` = ফেব্রুয়ারি) and weekdays are `Week-1` to `Week-6` (`星期一` = সোমবার ... `星期六` = শনিবার)!

### 1.2 Solve the Hard Points Early for Bangla Speakers
1. **The 4 Mandarin Tones + Neutral Tone (সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ ধাপ):**
   Changing pitch changes the word completely (`mā` মা vs `mǎ` ঘোড়া vs `mà` গালি):
   - **1st Tone (`1` / `ˉ`):** High & steady (`mā / ma1`) — একটানা উঁচু ও সমতল সুর (`মা—`)
   - **2nd Tone (`2` / `ˊ`):** Rising like asking *"হুঁ?"* (`má / ma2`) — নিচ থেকে উপরে ওঠা প্রশ্নবোধক সুর
   - **3rd Tone (`3` / `ˇ`):** Low dip then slight rise (`mǎ / ma3`) — গলায় নিচে নেমে হালকা উপরে ওঠা সুর
   - **4th Tone (`4` / `ˋ`):** Sharp falling command (`mà / ma4`) — উপর থেকে হঠাৎ জোরে নিচে নামা সুর
   - **Neutral Tone (`5` / light):** Short unstressed tap (`ma` 吗)
   - **Tone Sandhi Rules (`变调`):** Two 3rd tones together shift the first to 2nd tone (`你好 nǐ hǎo` ➔ spoken `ní hǎo`), plus `不 (bù)` & `一 (yī)` shifts.

2. **Pinyin Initials & Finals Trap Map (Bangla vs English Guessing):**

   | Pinyin | True Sound Tip in Bangla | Example | Bangla Phonetic (`bn_pron`) |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | **`zh`** | জিহ্বা উল্টে মোটা 'চ/জ়' (কোনো ফুঁ নয়) | `中国` (*Zhōngguó*) | `zhong1 guo2 · চোংকুও` |
   | **`ch`** | জিহ্বা উল্টে বাতাসের ঝাপটাসহ 'ছ' | `吃饭` (*chīfàn*) | `chi1 fan4 · ছি ফান্` |
   | **`sh`** | জিহ্বা উল্টে মোটা 'শ' | `老师` (*lǎoshī*) | `lao3 shi1 · লাওশি` |
   | **`r`** | জিহ্বা উল্টে 'র/ঝ়' মাঝামাঝি গুঞ্জন | `人` (*rén*) | `ren2 · রেন্` |
   | **`q`** | দাঁতের পেছনে জিহ্বা রেখে তীক্ষ্ণ 'ছ্‌+হ' | `请问` (*qǐngwèn*) | `qing3 wen4 · ছিং ওয়েন্` |
   | **`x`** | হাসিমুখে নরম 'স্‌+শ' আওয়াজ | `谢谢` (*xièxie*) | `xie4 xie · সিয়েসিয়ে` |
   | **`j`** | নরম তালু থেকে 'জ্/চ্' (ফুঁ ছাড়া) | `今天` (*jīntiān*) | `jin1 tian1 · চিন্ থিয়ান্` |
   | **`c`** | জোরালো 'ৎস্' (ফুঁ সহ) | `菜` (*cài*) | `cai4 · ৎসাই` |
   | **`z`** | নরম 'দ্‌জ় / ৎস্' (ফুঁ ছাড়া) | `再见` (*zàijiàn*) | `zai4 jian4 · দ্জাই চিয়ান্` |
   | **`ü (v)`** | ঠোঁট গোল করে 'ই+উ' | `女 / 绿` (*nǚ / lǜ*) | `nv3 · ন্যু` |

3. **`bn_pron` Script Purity Compliance:**
   - All `bn_pron` fields strictly follow [`BnPronScriptPurityTest.kt`](file:///e:/Dev_Nayem/audio_book_library/android/app/src/test/java/com/gigabyte/bookstore/learning/BnPronScriptPurityTest.kt) (`0x0980..0x09FF` + ASCII `0x20..0x7E` + middot `·`), using clear numbered Pinyin + pure Bengali bridge: e.g., `"ni3 hao3 · নি হাও"`.

## 3. Curriculum Syllabus Outline

### Bundle 1: Standard HSK 1 + Pinyin Stage 0 (`spoken_chinese_standard_hsk1.json`, 16 Parts × 4 Lessons = 64 Lessons, 512 Items)
- **Unit 1–2 (Stage 0 Tone & Pinyin Bootcamp):** 4 Tones contrast (`mā má mǎ mà`), Tone Sandhi (`你好`), tricky initials (`zh/ch/sh/r` vs `j/q/x` vs `z/c/s`), finals & `ü`.
- **Unit 3:** Greetings, Politeness & Respect (`您好, 谢谢, 不客气, 对不起`).
- **Unit 4:** Identity, Nationality & Introductions (`我叫…, 我是孟加拉国人`).
- **Unit 5:** Numbers 0–100, Phone Numbers, Large Unit `万` (10,000) & Measure Words (`个, 本`).
- **Unit 6:** Dates, Weekdays (`星期一–日`), Months & Telling Time (`几点了？`).
- **Unit 7:** Family & People (`爸爸, 妈妈, 哥哥, 妹妹, 朋友`).
- **Unit 8:** Halal Food, Restaurant Ordering & Drinks (`清真菜, 不吃猪肉, 喝茶/水`).
- **Unit 9:** Shopping, Prices & Bargaining (`多少钱？ 太贵了！ 便宜一点`).
- **Unit 10:** Transport, Taxi & Directions (`去机场, 地铁站怎么走？`).
- **Unit 11:** Daily Routine & Locations (`在家, 去公司/大学, 几点上班`).
- **Unit 12:** Weather, Seasons & Health Basics (`今天很热, 下雨了, 身体不舒服`).
- **Unit 13:** Likes, Hobbies & Abilities (`喜欢, 会说汉语, 想去北京`).
- **Unit 14:** Possession, Demonstration & Location (`的, 这/那, 里/外/上/下`).
- **Unit 15:** Past (`了`) vs Future (`要 / 想`) & Negative (`不 / 没`).
- **Unit 16:** HSKK Beginner Oral Mock Role-Plays & Review.

### Bundle 2–4: HSK 2, HSK 3, HSK 4 + Specialist Pro (Yiwu/Canton Trade, Engineering & CSC Scholarship)
Each follows the proven 16-part × 4-lesson × 8-item = **512 items/level** structure (`zh-hsk2-`, `zh-hsk3-`, `zh-hsk4-`, `zh-sp-`), enabling deep domain mastery for Bangladeshi importers, China engineering projects, and CSC scholarship students.
