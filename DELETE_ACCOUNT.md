# Account & Data Deletion Request
### বাংলা অডিওবুক ও স্পোকেন কোর্স (Bangla Audiobook & Spoken Course)

**Last Updated:** November 18, 2026

At **Bangla Audiobook & Spoken Course** (developed by Team Gigabyte), we respect your privacy and provide a simple, transparent process to delete your account and any associated personal data at any time.

---

### How to Request Account Deletion

You can request the complete deletion of your account and associated personal data by emailing our support team:

- **Support Email:** [teamgigabyte10@gmail.com](mailto:teamgigabyte10@gmail.com)
- **Email Subject:** `Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email]`
- **Required Information:**
  1. Your registered email address.
  2. Registered phone number (if provided during payment/registration).

*Note: Your request will be verified and permanently processed within 48 to 72 hours.*

---

### What Data Will Be Deleted Permanently:
When your account deletion is processed:
1. **User Profile & Credentials:** Your registered email address, name, and profile document in Cloud Firestore.
2. **Device Identifiers:** The unique device identifier (`ANDROID_ID`) linked to your account for single-device licensing.
3. **App Progress & Learning Records:** Your bookmarks, reading progress, course completions, and vocabulary history.
4. **Referral Records:** Any referral links or points linked to your profile.

---

### What Data May Be Retained:
- **Transaction Records:** If you made a payment via mobile banking (bKash/Nagad/Rocket), basic transaction IDs and payment approval records may be retained in anonymized accounting records solely for legal compliance, tax, and anti-fraud auditing purposes. No personal identity or device tokens are retained beyond the legally required retention period.
