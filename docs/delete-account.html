At Bangla Audiobook & Spoken Course (developed by Team Gigabyte), we respect your right to control your personal data. You have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and associated personal information at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion:

To submit an account and data deletion request, send an email to our support desk:

Email: teamgigabyte10@gmail.com

Subject Line: Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email]

Required Information:

  • Your registered email address.
  • Your phone number (if linked with your account/orders).

Requests are verified and permanently executed within 48 to 72 business hours.

What Data Will Be Deleted Permanently

Upon verification of your request, the following data is permanently purged from our Cloud Firestore database and storage servers:

  • User Account Information: Your registered email address, name, password hash, and profile document.
  • Device Binding Tokens: Unique device IDs (ANDROID_ID) assigned for single-device licensing.
  • Learning Progress & Bookmarks: Your audio bookmarks, playback history, word quiz scores, and saved vocabulary items.
  • Referral Details: Any referral associations tied to your account.

Data Retention Policy

  • Financial Auditing Records: If you made digital content purchases or manual payments (e.g. bKash / Nagad / Rocket transaction IDs), basic non-personal ledger records may be kept strictly for accounting, taxation, and anti-fraud auditing purposes as mandated by applicable laws.
