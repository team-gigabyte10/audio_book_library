Team Gigabyte operates the বাংলা অডিওবুক ও স্পোকেন কোর্স (com.gigabyte.bookstore) mobile application. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information for anyone using our Service.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Account Data: Your name, email address, and optional phone number when registering an account.
  • Device Identifiers: We collect a hardware identifier (ANDROID_ID) solely to prevent unauthorized account sharing across multiple devices and ensure single-device licensing.
  • App Usage Data: Audio playback progress, bookmarked items, quiz scores, and course progress stored locally and synced via Firebase.
  • Payment Verification: When manual payment methods (e.g. bKash, Nagad) are used, transaction IDs provided by the user are submitted to verify course activation.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and personalize the educational features and audio playback.
  • To authenticate users and verify single-device license terms.
  • To process and approve user-requested course activations.
  • To provide timely customer support.

3. Data Protection and Encryption

All user information transmitted between your mobile device and our Cloud Firestore servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party marketing services.

4. Account and Data Deletion

Users have the full right to delete their account and associated data at any time. For detailed instructions on how to request deletion, please visit our Account Deletion Page or email us directly at teamgigabyte10@gmail.com.
